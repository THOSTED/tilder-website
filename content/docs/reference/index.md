## Name

reference - every construct, key and option, to look up

The guide tells a story; the reference answers a question. Each page
here covers one part of tilder in full, with its defaults and its exact
behaviour, so you can come to it from a search or a link and leave with
the detail you needed.

## The pages

[The Markdown reference](docs/reference/markdown) shows every construct of
the dialect tilder reads, first as source, then rendered on the page
itself: sections, blocks, entries, inline markup, links and images.

[Configuration](docs/reference/configuration) lists every key of
`site.toml`, table by table, with its default and an example.

[The text mirror](docs/reference/text-mirror) describes the plain-text
twin of every page: 75 columns, ASCII, its coloured version, and the host
that serves it.

[SEO](docs/reference/seo) covers what the build writes for search engines
and link previews, and the checks it runs on titles and descriptions.

[Feeds and images](docs/reference/feeds-and-images) covers the RSS feeds,
the iCalendar files, the icons and the link-preview image.

[The command line](docs/reference/cli) gives every option of `build.py`,
its errors, its warnings and its exit codes.

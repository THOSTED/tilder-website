## Name

tilder 1.6.0 - pages, a site-wide search, hours and sessions, categories

tilder 1.6.0 is out, and the site is built with it. The image is
`ghcr.io/thosted/tilder:1.6.0`. A site that sets none of the new keys
builds as before, but for the text mirror's cards (below).

## Paged lists

`per_page` on a collection shows its `{posts}` or `{past}` list N cards
at a time: page n at `/blog/n`, each with its own title, description,
canonical URL and sitemap entry, and a `previous · page n of m · next`
line. `item_prefix = "-"` moves the items to `/blog/-/<slug>`, so page
numbers and items never meet; the RSS `guid` keeps the old URL
([configuration](docs/reference/configuration)).

## A search for the whole site

A section marked `{search}` lists every post, event and member, each
card carrying its words. The theme's script searches them in the page -
no request, no form - and shows `[search] limit` results at a time;
without JavaScript, every card is shown, and the text mirror is the whole
index, for `grep` ([configuration](docs/reference/configuration)).

## Hours and sessions for events

`time: 14:00-15:00` gives an event its hours; without it, it lasts whole
days, and `end` still spans several. `session-1`, `session-2`... give one
event several iCalendar entries: a stand all day and a workshop at 14:00,
on one page ([events](docs/content-types/event)).

## Several categories, and tags

A member's `category` takes several (`admin mentor`), and
`default_category = ""` leaves the category everyone has untagged
([members](docs/content-types/member)). A post adds `tags:` to its `tag`
([posts](docs/content-types/post)).

## The text mirror's cards

A card is framed down its left side, `,` `|` `'`, so the cards of a list
read apart; tags that do not fit beside the title go under it. Every link
now shows where it goes - an internal one as its path, `/blog/` - so a
reader always finds the next thing to curl
([the text mirror](docs/reference/text-mirror)).

## Three colours for the terminal

`[text]` gains `link` (URLs and paths, underlined) and `muted` (rules
and frames), beside `accent`, and a colour may be a bright twin
(`bright-cyan`) or `dim`: tags and titles stand out, links a level
quieter ([configuration](docs/reference/configuration)).

## The 404 at any depth

The server answers any missing URL with the 404, at any depth: its links
now start at the root, so it keeps its style, and its navigation no
longer grows `blog/blog/`.
